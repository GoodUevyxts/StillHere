    The entirely unnecessary rulebook

    Level up: earn 100 accepted clicks per level. The pebble changes its shape, colour and search position at every level. It shrinks to a minimum 44px target and gains up to 12 camouflage stones. Search difficulty caps at level 13; appearances keep changing. The pale notch identifies your pebble. Reveal and keyboard focus can help at any time.

    Four ways to play: tap the real rock, catch the shifting inspection button, hold for three seconds, or start a three-second deep inspection. Each completion earns exactly one click and one independent movement chance. Deep inspection works without holding. Other scoring actions pause during a hold or deep inspection.

    Slow down: the seventh attempt within two seconds—including decoys—resets the current level’s click progress. Cooldowns last 15, 30, 60, then at most 120 seconds. Attempts during a cooldown earn nothing and do not extend it. Lifetime clicks, earned levels, waiting time and badges remain. Reloading does not clear a cooldown.

    Rare movement: every accepted click has a 0.10% chance (1 in 1,000). A win makes the rock visibly wobble, records a movement and unlocks a badge. Repositioning between search levels is separate from a rare movement. There is no guaranteed win or limit at 10,000 clicks.

    Pointless errors: after at least 20 accepted clicks since the last event, each new accepted click has a 6% chance to trigger a fictional pebble error. A rare movement takes priority. The error stays until you clear one row in Pebble Blocks (10 points), or complete its accessible text challenge. A repair adds 100 main-game points. Puzzle points and your main score are separate. Play later pauses the puzzle; it does not remove the error.

    Patience: time accumulates only while this tab is visible, including cooldowns. No offline rewards. Data is stored locally; clearing browser storage removes it. This is a casual game, not a cheat-proof leaderboard. Previous automatic inspection counts are kept in your report, separately from new game clicks.
